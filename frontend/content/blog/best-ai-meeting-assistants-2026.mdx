## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most accurate AI meeting assistant in 2026?
Accuracy depends on both the acoustic ASR model and the underlying LLM reasoning engine. Platforms utilizing modern Whisper architectures (such as [Faster-Whisper](/blog/faster-whisper-explained)) combined with multi-model synthesis (like MeetMind AI and Fathom) deliver high transcription accuracy and reliable speaker attribution compared to older speech-to-text engines.

### Do AI meeting assistants require a bot to join the call?
No. While tools like Fireflies and Otter rely on calendar participant bots, platforms like MeetMind AI and Krisp operate asynchronously or via native audio interception, eliminating the need for third-party bots in your conference room.

### Can AI meeting assistants automatically create Jira or Linear tasks?
Yes. Platforms with structured task parsing can detect actionable commitments and export them directly into project management systems like Jira, Linear, Asana, and Trello. For practical steps, read our guide on [Turning Meeting Transcripts into Action Items](/blog/turn-meeting-transcripts-into-action-items-using-ai).

### How do AI meeting assistants improve team productivity?
By automating note-taking, transcription, and task follow-ups, AI meeting tools save team members 2 to 4 hours per week. Read our deep-dive on [How to Write Better Meeting Minutes](/blog/how-to-write-better-meeting-minutes) and [Best Practices for Leveraging AI Summaries](/blog/best-practices-leveraging-ai-summaries).

### Are free AI meeting assistants safe for business use?
Free tiers of some consumer-grade tools may include terms of service that allow anonymized data usage for model training. For confidential business calls, always verify that the vendor guarantees zero model training, SOC 2 compliance, and end-to-end data encryption.

### How much do AI meeting assistants cost?
Pricing typically ranges from free tiers (Fathom, MeetMind AI) to $10–$20 per user/month for professional business tiers (Fireflies, Otter). MeetMind AI also offers a ₹299 one-time plan for 100 meetings, while enterprise suites like Microsoft Copilot start at $30/user/month with annual commitments.
