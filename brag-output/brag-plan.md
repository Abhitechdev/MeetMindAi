# Brag Plan: MeetMind AI

## What is this app?
MeetMind AI transforms meeting audio and video recordings into executive summaries, verbatim transcripts, key decisions, and action items in seconds without bots joining calls.

## The angle
Stop inviting intrusive bots to your calendar. Record locally, drop the file, and get instant executive-level intelligence in under 45 seconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep obsidian dark mode erupts with a purple/blue aurora glow. "Transform Meetings Into Actionable Intelligence" slams in with a high-contrast pill badge reading "MeetMind AI 2.0".

## Key moments (the middle)
- Drag & drop simulation of `Q3-Product-Sync.m4a` into the sleek glassmorphism upload container.
- GPU Whisper speech-to-text progress bar filling in real-time ("32 mins transcribed in 4.2s").
- Instant tripartite output reveal:
  - 📝 Executive Summary
  - ⚡ 3 Key Decisions popping in sequentially
  - ✅ 3 Action Items with real assignees checking off on the beat

## Outro / punchline
"Zero bots. Pure intelligence. Try it free at meetmindai.com."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: User drops `Q3-Product-Sync.m4a` into the upload zone.
Key action: Whisper transcription and LLM executive engine process the file.
Result: The 3 core intelligence pillars (Summary, Decisions, Action Items) render with pristine typography.
