# MeetMind AI Demo Script

**Target Duration:** 90 seconds

**[0:00 - 0:10] Problem**
*(Visual: A cluttered project management board or a very long Google Doc of meeting notes.)*
**Voiceover:** "We spend hours in meetings, but the decisions and commitments often get lost in long transcripts or scattered docs. You know a decision was made, but you can't remember when or why."

**[0:10 - 0:20] Upload Meeting**
*(Visual: MeetMind UI landing page. The user drags and drops a meeting audio file into the Dropzone.)*
**Voiceover:** "MeetMind AI fixes this. You simply upload your meeting audio..."

**[0:20 - 0:35] Meeting Intelligence**
*(Visual: Fast-forward extraction. The UI populates with structured entities: a "Decisions" tab, a "Commitments" tab. The user clicks into a decision.)*
**Voiceover:** "Instead of just a summary, MeetMind structurally extracts the actual intelligence. It identifies the exact decisions made, the open action items, and who committed to what."

**[0:35 - 0:50] Ask cross-meeting question**
*(Visual: The user navigates to the 'Global Search' bar and types: "What did we decide about the payment integration across Q3?")*
**Voiceover:** "But the real power is persistent memory. You can ask complex, cross-meeting questions that span your entire organization's history."

**[0:50 - 0:65] Show change/history**
*(Visual: The AI response highlights a 'SUPERSEDED' decision and a 'CURRENT' decision regarding the payment gateway.)*
**Voiceover:** "It doesn't just find keywords; it detects change. Here, it recognizes that we initially decided on PayPal in July, but that decision was superseded by Stripe in August."

**[0:65 - 0:75] Show relationship reasoning**
*(Visual: The UI shows a relationship graph or bullet points linking 'Sarah', 'Payment Integration', and 'Stripe'.)*
**Voiceover:** "It also understands relationships. It knows Sarah owns the payment integration because she took the action item in a completely different meeting."

**[0:75 - 0:90] Show evidence + evaluation result**
*(Visual: The user clicks a citation link `[1]` in the AI's answer, and it jumps directly to the highlighted transcript snippet from the August meeting.)*
**Voiceover:** "Best of all, every claim is grounded in evidence. No hallucinations. Just click the source, and see exactly what was said. MeetMind AI: Your organization's persistent memory."
