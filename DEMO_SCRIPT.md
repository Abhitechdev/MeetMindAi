# MeetMind AI — Demo Script

**Target duration:** 60–90 seconds  
**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover  
**Rule:** No fabricated results — only real UI shown

---

## Script

---

### [0–10 sec] The Problem

**Show:** Blank screen / title card

**Say:**
> "Most meeting AI tools stop at transcription.
> They summarize what happened today — but they can't tell you
> what your team decided last month, who committed to what,
> or what changed since your last meeting.
> MeetMind fixes that."

---

### [10–25 sec] Upload a Meeting

**Show:** MeetMind landing page → navigate to 'New Meeting'

**Say:**
> "Drop in a meeting recording — audio or video, up to 100MB."

**Show:** Drag and drop audio file onto the dropzone. File appears selected.

**Show:** Click 'Process Meeting'

**Say:**
> "MeetMind transcribes the audio, identifies speakers,
> and runs structured extraction — decisions, action items,
> commitments, people, and relationships."

---

### [25–40 sec] Meeting Intelligence

**Show:** Meeting detail page loading. Show the structured output:
- Executive summary
- Decisions panel (with confidence score)
- Commitments panel (with person and status)
- Action items panel

**Say:**
> "Every meeting generates a structured intelligence record —
> not just a summary. Decisions are tracked with status and confidence.
> Commitments are linked to the person who made them.
> Everything is stored and queryable."

---

### [40–55 sec] Cross-Meeting Query

**Show:** Global chat interface (global-chat-bot or memory query)

**Type:** "What did we decide about payment integration?"

**Show:** Response appears with decision record, confidence, and meeting citation.

**Say:**
> "Ask anything across your full meeting history.
> MeetMind searches your stored memory,
> reasons across relevant meetings,
> and cites its sources — so you can verify every answer."

**Type:** "What commitments are still open?"

**Show:** Commitment list with person names and status.

---

### [55–70 sec] Change Detection

**Show:** Same query interface

**Type:** "What changed since our last meeting?"

**Show:** Response showing decision status changes or new commitments since previous meeting.

**Say:**
> "It tracks what changed across meetings — decisions that were
> reversed, commitments that were completed,
> and issues that keep recurring."

---

### [70–85 sec] Evidence + Verdict

**Show:** One response with visible meeting citation (e.g., "Source: Payment Sync — 2026-01-15")

**Say:**
> "Every answer is grounded in evidence from your actual meetings.
> No hallucinated facts. No invented context.
> Just what your team actually said, decided, and committed to."

**Show:** Title card: "MeetMind AI — meetmindai.co.in"

---

## Recording Notes

- Use real meeting data if possible. Synthetic placeholder data is acceptable if labeled.
- Show all three panels: decisions, commitments, action items.
- Show at least one cross-meeting query with a visible meeting citation in the response.
- Keep cursor movements smooth — avoid jerky navigation.
- Resolution: 1920×1080 preferred. 1280×720 minimum.
- File format: MP4 (H.264) for portfolio embedding.
