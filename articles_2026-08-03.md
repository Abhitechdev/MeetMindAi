## 1. Faster Whisper Explained: The Engine Behind Real-Time Meeting AI

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) has undergone a massive transformation with OpenAI's Whisper, but deploying Whisper in production at scale has historically been hindered by high compute and memory requirements. 

Enter **Faster-Whisper**—a reimagined implementation built on top of **CTranslate2**. By utilizing integer quantization (8-bit and 4-bit weights) and highly optimized C++ GEMM kernels, Faster-Whisper achieves up to **4x inference speedups** while cutting memory consumption by over **50%**, with virtually zero degradation in Word Error Rate (WER). For real-time meeting intelligence platforms like MeetingMindAI, this optimization makes low-latency transcription viable on standard edge servers and consumer-grade GPUs.

## 2. Edge vs. Cloud Speech Recognition: Privacy & Latency Tradeoffs

When designing meeting intelligence architectures, engineering teams face a crucial architectural fork: process audio on edge devices or stream to cloud APIs?

Cloud endpoints offer immense throughput and effortless scaling, but they introduce network latency, variable egress costs, and corporate compliance friction regarding confidential corporate discussions. On-device or private edge inference eliminates data transfer security risks, ensures compliance with strict GDPR/HIPAA standards, and provides sub-200ms latency for live speaker feedback. As quantized neural architectures mature, hybrid processing—where speech recognition is executed locally and secondary synthesis is handled centrally—is rapidly becoming the standard enterprise topology.

## 3. Action Item Extraction: How LLMs Parse Transcripts into Trackable Tasks

A 60-minute meeting transcript can easily surpass 8,000 tokens of raw, conversational text filled with filler words, interruptions, and fragmented sentences. Extracting high-fidelity action items requires more than naive summarization.

Modern meeting pipelines employ targeted Few-Shot Prompting and constrained JSON decoding to identify implicit commitments ("*I'll push the PR by end of day*"), map responsibilities to specific speaker clusters, and calculate urgency scores. These structured outputs feed directly into downstream project trackers like Jira, Linear, and Asana—closing the loop between discussion and execution without human administrative overhead.
